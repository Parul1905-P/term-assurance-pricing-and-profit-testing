# Term Assurance Pricing and Profit Testing Model

An Excel-based Actuarial model developed to calculate premiums and evaluate profitability of term assurance product.
This project applies concepts from CM1 (Actuarial Mathematics) to life insurance pricing, Expected Present Value (EPV) calculations and Profit Testing.


## Features


- **Assumptions:** Defines key pricing inputs, including entry age, policy term, sum assured, interest rate, mortality loading, expenses, commissions, and target profit margin.
- **Mortality Table:** Uses the IALM 2012–14 Ultimate mortality table to incorporate age-specific mortality assumptions.
- **Claims EPV:** Calculates the expected present value of death benefit payments using mortality probabilities and discounting.
- **Premium Calculation:** Estimates the annual premium based on the present value of expected benefits, expenses, and the target profit margin.
- **Expense Calculations:** Incorporates acquisition expenses, renewal expenses, variable expenses, and commission assumptions.
- **Profit Testing:** Projects expected cash flows over the policy term to evaluate the profitability of the insurance product.
- **Sensitivity Analysis:** Examines the impact of changes in key assumptions on premium estimates and projected profitability.

## Key Assumptions

- Entry Age: 30 years
- Policy Term: 20 years
- Sum Assured: ₹1,00,00,000
- Premium Frequency: Annual
- Mortality Table: IALM 2012–14 Ultimate
- Pricing Interest Rate: 6.50%
- Mortality Loading: 10%
- Acquisition Expense: ₹500
- Renewal Expense: ₹150
- Variable Expenses: 5% of Premium
- First-Year Commission: 20%
- Renewal Commission: 5%
- Target Profit Margin: 10%

These assumptions are illustrative and can be modified to explore different pricing scenarios.
